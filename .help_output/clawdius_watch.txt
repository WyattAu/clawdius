Watch files for changes and trigger auto-analysis

Usage: clawdius watch [OPTIONS] [PATH]

Arguments:
  [PATH]  Path to watch (file or directory) [default: .]

Options:
      --ignore <IGNORE>            Patterns to ignore (comma-separated)
      --auto-analyze               Enable auto-analysis on changes
      --debounce-ms <DEBOUNCE_MS>  Debounce interval in milliseconds [default: 500]
  -v, --verbose                    Enable verbose output
  -h, --help                       Print help
